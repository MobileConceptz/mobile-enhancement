⭐ Add to Home Screen

Android

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu in Chrome.
  2. Select Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install.
  4. Our logo will appear like an app.

iPhone

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Select Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add.
  4. The Mobile Enhancement icon will appear on your Home Screen.
